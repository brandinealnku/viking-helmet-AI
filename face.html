About

How This Version Works

BlazeFace detects a face box and landmarks. The app watches where your face sits in the frame, estimates whether you moved left or right, and measures whether your face appears closer to the camera.

Move Left

Shift your face toward the left side of the frame to move backward through the nav.

Move Right

Shift your face toward the right side of the frame to move forward through the nav.

Lean In

Move closer to the camera and hold briefly to confirm your current selection.

Centered Neutral State

When your face is centered and steady, the system stays in a ready/tracking mode.
