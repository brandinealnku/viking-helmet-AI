Live Crowd Counter

Point your phone toward the audience and this demo will detect and count how many people are visible in the frame in real time.

How to use it

  1. 1.Tap Start Rear Camera.
  2. 2.Allow camera access when prompted.
  3. 3.Turn your phone toward the crowd.
  4. 4.Watch the live people count update on screen.

Detection modes

Balanced gives a steady mix of speed and accuracy.
Fast is lighter for older phones.
Precise is a bit more selective and can reduce false positives.

What it demonstrates

• Real-time person detection in the browser
• Rear mobile camera support
• Live count of visible people
• On-device AI processing with TensorFlow.js
• Snapshot capture for demos and events
