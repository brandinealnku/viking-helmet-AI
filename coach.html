Interactive Demo

Laugh to unlock Digital Viking mode.

This demo uses the camera and a browser-based AI model to watch for a strong laugh pattern. When the laugh score stays high long enough, the unlock sequence appears on screen.

How to use it

  1. 1. Select Start Camera & AI.
  2. 2. Look at the camera and laugh naturally.
  3. 3. Once unlocked, create and download a share card if you want to save the moment.

This is a playful demo rather than scientific emotion detection. A strong smile, animated talking, or other expressive face movement may also raise the score.

Best results: one face in frame, clear lighting, and a steady camera view.
