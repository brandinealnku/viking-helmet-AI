NKU Norse AI Filter

This rewritten version is designed to reduce overlay jitter by separating the AI detection loop from the render loop, then smoothing the face box before drawing the filter.

Try it out

  1. 1. Click Start Camera & AI.
  2. 2. Choose Glasses, Helmet, or No filter.
  3. 3. Capture a stable Norse selfie.

What changed

• Face detection runs on a timed interval instead of every render frame.
• Overlay position is eased toward the target box for smoother motion.
• The last good face box is briefly held when the model momentarily loses track.
• Only one canvas is rendered for a cleaner, more stable effect.

Tap “Start Camera & AI” to launch the demo.

Best results: bright lighting, one face at a time, and recent Chrome or Edge.
