NKU Norse Expression AI

This demo uses JavaScript, TensorFlow.js, and your browser’s camera to detect a very simple facial expression label in real time (Neutral, Smiling, Surprised). Everything runs locally in your browser – no uploads, no server, no Python.

Try it out

  1. 1. Click Start Camera & AI and give your browser camera access.
  2. 2. Look at the camera and try a neutral face, a big smile, and a surprised “O” mouth.
  3. 3. Watch the expression label update at the top of the video.

What you’ll learn

• How websites safely request camera access using getUserMedia.
• How <canvas> lets us draw graphics and overlays on top of live video.
• How a lightweight model like BlazeFace (TensorFlow.js) can track faces and power simple AI UX patterns – all without sending any data anywhere.

Tap “Start Camera & AI” to launch the demo.

Best results: Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge, HTTPS, and bright lighting.

Your data stays entirely on your device – nothing is uploaded or stored. This is a hands-on, low-pressure way to explore modern AI user experiences in the browser. Enjoy!
