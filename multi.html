Interactive AI Crowd Mirror

This upgraded version recognizes multiple people, labels each person, shows a live crowd count, and triggers a celebration moment when a group enters the frame.

How to use it

  1. 1.Select Start Camera & AI.
  2. 2.Have at least 3 people step into view.
  3. 3.Watch the celebration trigger, countdown, and auto-snapshot.
  4. 4.Use Auto Crowd or choose a specific overlay mode.

What it demonstrates

• Multi-face detection with BlazeFace
• Multi-person segmentation with BodyPix
• Auto-assigned overlays for groups
• Crowd counting and face labels
• Countdown-based capture moment for events and demos
