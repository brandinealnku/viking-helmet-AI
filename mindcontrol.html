About

How the Demo Works

The app estimates a face mesh from the webcam and measures relative changes in eyes, brows, and head angle. It first learns a neutral baseline, then looks for expression changes that are strong enough to count as intentional navigation commands.

Eyebrow Raise

A noticeable brow lift compared with your neutral face moves the highlight forward.

Head Tilt

Tilting left moves back. Tilting right moves forward. The model ignores tiny natural wobble.

Deliberate Blink

A slightly longer blink acts like a confirm/select action while filtering out most normal blinks.

Neutral Calibration

The first couple of seconds are used to learn your natural brow position and eye openness.
