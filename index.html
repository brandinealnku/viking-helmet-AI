Browser-based face filter

NKU Norse AI Filter

A live face filter built with JavaScript, TensorFlow.js, and Canva overlays. Everything runs locally in your browser — no Python, no server, and no login.

How to try it

  • 1. Click Start Camera & AI and allow camera access.
  • 2. Choose a filter: Glasses, Helmet, or No filter.
  • 3. Capture a selfie, preview it, and decide if you want to email it to your instructor.

What students learn

• How modern sites request camera access safely with getUserMedia.
• How <canvas> layers graphics on top of live video.
• How client-side AI models (BodyPix + BlazeFace via TensorFlow.js) can power real-time background replacement and AR effects without sending data to a backend.

Tap “Start Camera & AI” to begin.
Best experience: recent browser, HTTPS, and good lighting.
