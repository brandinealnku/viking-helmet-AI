Screen recorder demo

Record the full screen while keeping the camera on.

This demo combines screen share and webcam into one recording. It draws both into a single canvas, then records that canvas as a downloadable video.

How it works

1. Start screen + camera
2. Choose your full screen in the browser picker
3. Click record
4. Stop and download

System audio support depends on the browser and which share option you choose.

Click “Start Screen + Camera” to begin.
Best results: choose Entire Screen and allow camera + microphone access.
