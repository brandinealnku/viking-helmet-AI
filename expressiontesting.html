AI customer mood panel

Real-time mood scores,
right in the browser.

This prototype uses a lightweight face model in the browser to estimate rough customer mood scores – positivity, focus, and energy – from live video. All AI runs locally; only numeric scores are computed. No images are uploaded or stored.

Try it out

  1. Click Start Camera & AI and grant camera access.
  2. Look at the camera, smile, lean in, or move around a bit.
  3. Watch the Customer Mood Panel react in real time.

Important notes

• Scores are illustrative only – this is a design prototype, not a scientific tool.
• Uses a face detection model (BlazeFace) plus simple heuristics and smoothing.
• In a production system, these scores would feed a customer analytics dashboard (NPS trends, session replay, A/B tests, etc.) without storing raw video.

Tap “Start Camera & AI” to launch the demo.
