About

How the Expression Engine Works

The app uses a live face mesh to estimate brow lift, eye openness, and the angle between your eyes. It first learns your neutral facial baseline, then reacts only when a change is strong enough to count as intentional.

Neutral Calibration

The system spends the first moment learning your natural brow position, eye openness, and head angle.

Raised Brows = Advance

A measurable brow lift relative to baseline becomes a forward navigation command.

Tilt = Direction

Tilt left to move backward. Tilt right to move forward. Tiny wobble is ignored.

Blink = Confirm

A slightly longer blink works like a selection click while filtering out most normal blinks.
