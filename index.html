😎 NKU Norse AI Filter – Modern UX

JavaScript grabs your webcam, TensorFlow.js + BlazeFace detect your face, and a Canva PNG overlay is drawn in real time on <canvas>. All in the browser — no backend, no Python.

Tap “Start Camera & AI” to begin.
Tip: Use a modern browser over HTTPS, then allow camera access.

When you capture a selfie, you’ll be asked if you want to save it to your NKU_AI_Selfies folder. Your browser will download a PNG — students can drag it into that folder or upload it to Canvas/Teams.

Under the hood: this demo uses getUserMedia for the webcam, BlazeFace for face detection, and ctx.drawImage() to position a Canva-designed PNG relative to your face box.
