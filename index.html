NKU Norse AI Filter

A live face filter built with JavaScript, TensorFlow.js, and Canva overlays. Everything runs locally in your browser — no Python, no server, and no login.

How it works for you

  • 1. Click Start Camera & AI and allow camera access.
  • 2. Pick a filter: Glasses, Helmet, or No filter.
  • 3. Capture a selfie, preview it, and decide if you want to email it to your instructor.

Use this as a template for your own AI-powered web experiences: replace overlays, change the prompts, or wire it into a class assignment.

What this demo teaches

• How modern websites access hardware safely using getUserMedia.
• How <canvas> lets you layer graphics on top of live video.
• How client-side AI (TensorFlow.js + BlazeFace) can power real-time effects without sending data to a server.

Tap “Start Camera & AI” to begin.
Tip: Use a modern browser over HTTPS, with decent lighting.
