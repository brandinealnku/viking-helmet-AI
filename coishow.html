Griffin Hall background. Automatic Viking helmet.

This version keeps the experience focused and clean. The live camera view is composited into Griffin Hall, and the helmet is placed automatically on the user’s head with no extra visual clutter.

What changed

Griffin Hall now fills the full stage first, and only the person is drawn on top using segmentation. That makes the background replacement much stronger than the earlier version.

Best results

Use even lighting, keep one face clearly in frame, and stand far enough back so the top of the head is visible. Busy lighting and very dark clothing can reduce segmentation quality.
