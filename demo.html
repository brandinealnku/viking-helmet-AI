How to demo it
  1. 1 Select Start Camera + AI and allow camera access.
  2. 2 Pick a presentation mode: Glasses, Helmet, Viking Face, or Griffin Only.
  3. 3 Use the live stage for your faculty/staff/student demo, then capture a branded snapshot.
What makes it interesting
  • Runs locally in the browser with getUserMedia, <canvas>, TensorFlow.js, BlazeFace, and BodyPix.
  • Keeps the experience campus-centered by replacing personal spaces with a Griffin Hall background.
  • Captured images now include the same cinematic overlay shown on the live feed.
  • Built for desktop, tablet, and mobile so people can try it quickly during events.
Privacy note: camera frames stay on the device. This page does not upload or store live camera data.
