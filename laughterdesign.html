Interactive laugh signal

A calmer, smarter browser AI experience.

This version is optimized around modern interaction expectations: fast feedback, local processing, clean state changes, and clear language about uncertainty. Instead of claiming exact emotion detection, it estimates whether the user is likely laughing and communicates that state clearly.

How it works

The demo looks for a combination of mouth openness, repeated mouth movement, and light face “bounce” over several frames. That reduces flicker and feels more intentional than a single-frame trigger.

Privacy note

Camera frames stay in the browser. This prototype does not upload or store your video. The output is only a live heuristic estimate for interaction design.
