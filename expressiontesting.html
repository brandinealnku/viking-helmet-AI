NKU Norse Expression AI

This demo uses JavaScript, TensorFlow.js, and your browser’s camera to estimate a very simple, playful facial expression label in real time (Neutral, Smiling, or Surprised). Everything runs locally in your browser – no uploads, no server.

Try it out

  1. 1. Click Start Camera & AI and grant camera access.
  2. 2. Try a neutral face, a big smile, and a surprised “O” mouth.
  3. 3. Watch the expression text update below the buttons.

Privacy & ethics

• Your camera feed is processed only on your device. No images are uploaded or stored.
• This is a rough, playful demo – not a psychological or medical tool.
• You can stop the camera and AI at any time using the Stop button.

Tap “Start Camera & AI” to launch the demo.

Best results: Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge, HTTPS, and bright, even lighting.
