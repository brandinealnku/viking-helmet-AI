Finger Countdown Camera

This version uses your camera and hand tracking to turn a finger countdown into a hands-free photo booth experience. Hold up 1 finger, then 2 fingers, then 3 fingers. The app will show CHEESE! and automatically take your picture.

How to use it

  1. 1. Tap Start Camera & AI.
  2. 2. Move one hand clearly into view with good lighting.
  3. 3. Hold up 1 finger, then 2, then 3.
  4. 4. When the screen says CHEESE!, the photo is captured automatically.

What it demonstrates

• Camera access with getUserMedia
• Real-time hand landmark detection in the browser
• Finger counting based on hand landmarks
• Canvas overlays and auto snapshot logic
