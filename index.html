NKU Norse AI Filter

This demo shows how far modern web tech has come — a live face-tracking filter built with JavaScript, TensorFlow.js, and custom Canva assets. Everything runs right in your browser. No Python. No server. No upload. Just pure, fast, client-side AI.

Try it out

  1. 1. Click Start Camera & AI and give your browser camera access.
  2. 2. Choose your look: Glasses, Helmet, or go with No filter (just you + Griffin Hall).
  3. 3. Snap a Norse-powered selfie and preview it instantly.

What you’ll learn

• How websites safely request camera access using getUserMedia.
• How <canvas> lets us draw filters on top of live video.
• How AI models like BodyPix + BlazeFace (TensorFlow.js) track faces and apply AR effects - all without sending any data anywhere.

Tap “Start Camera & AI” to launch the demo.

Best results: Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge, HTTPS, and bright lighting.
